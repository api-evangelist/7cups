# 7 Cups

> 7 Cups (7cups.com) is an on-demand emotional health and online therapy service backed by Y Combinator. It connects people with trained volunteer listeners for free, anonymous one-on-one chat, and matches members with licensed therapists for paid online therapy by messaging, audio, or video, alongside peer community forums and group support. It operates across 189 countries in 140 languages. 7 Cups is a consumer web and mobile product and publishes no public API at this time.
